About Me

I'm an aspiring Information Technology professional with a strong interest in software development, computer systems, networking, game development, animation, and digital creativity.

I enjoy building things from the ground up — whether it's a website, a management system, a game, or a solution to a technical problem. I also have hands-on experience with computer troubleshooting and maintenance, including diagnosing hardware problems, assembling computers, installing operating systems, configuring networks, and solving software-related issues.

Beyond development, I explore game creation, animation, video editing, and interactive storytelling, allowing me to combine technology with creativity. I use GitHub to manage, showcase, and launch my projects, keeping my work organized while continuously learning modern development practices.
